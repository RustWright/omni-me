## 🔴 `hidden_when` — a new catalogue mechanism, and why it had to exist
⚠️ **`transactions` is the FIRST catalogued table where a row can be soft-deleted.** Every query built its `WHERE` from model-supplied filters alone, so registering the entry as-is would have handed the assistant **deleted money to cite as evidence**. ⛔ Not a filter — "the model remembered to exclude deleted rows" is not a property worth having.
⛔ **Applied to FOUR paths, and the fourth is the one that gets missed:** `search`, `list`, `read`, the `list_types` count — **and `vector_store::sweep`**, a *separate read path*. A row hidden after being embedded has its chunks **deleted**, not skipped; skipping leaves it reachable by `knn_search` forever after the SQL verbs stopped returning it. ⚠️ `!= true` not `= false`: of the two failure modes, a row wrongly appearing gets noticed and a transaction silently vanishing does not.
