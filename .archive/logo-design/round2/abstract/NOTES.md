### ab-01-open-ring.svg — The Meaningful Gap (primary)
Concept: a thick blue ring with a deliberate ~44° break at the upper-right, holding a solid off-white "me" core.
Why it fits: the flagship idea — a complete-looking whole that is honestly open; no app fully encloses a life. Self-at-center in two-tone. Reads instantly at any size.
Con: the bare ring-with-gap is a common motif; leans on execution (gap placement, core weight) to feel intentional rather than generic.

### ab-02-open-spiral.svg — Spiral that never closes
Concept: one continuous blue stroke spiralling out from a small off-white seed core, ~2.5 turns, ending open.
Why it fits: growth / journaling over time accumulating around the self; the never-closing end is the meaningful-gap idea expressed as motion, not a notch.
Con: thinner outer coils can crowd; the loosest/most illustrative mark here, so it's borderline below ~40px.

### ab-03-vessel.svg — Vessel cradling the core
Concept: a bold blue cup — a ring open ~140° at the top — with a large off-white core nested inside it.
Why it fits: the app as a container/nest that holds your life; the wide top opening reads as "held, not sealed." Very legible, punchy at 32px.
Con: at small sizes it can read as a plain broken ring rather than specifically a "cup" until you notice the core sitting in it.

### ab-04-strata.svg — Concentric strata of self
Concept: three concentric gapped rings (inner ring in light `#7fb0ff` for depth) with staggered breaks, around an off-white core — like tree rings / accumulated layers of a life.
Why it fits: event-sourced accumulation over time; each staggered gap keeps the "never fully complete" theme; the one tint adds quiet depth.
Con: three thin rings muddy at 32px — this is a large-size showpiece, not a favicon.

### ab-05-interlock.svg — Interlocked wholeness
Concept: three overlapping blue rings (Borromean-style unity) meeting over a shared off-white core.
Why it fits: interconnected domains where no single ring holds it all — together they form the whole, self at the mutual center.
Con: true over/under weave isn't shown (flat overlap), and three thin strokes get busy small; borderline at 32px.

### ab-06-seed.svg — Germinating seed
Concept: a bold filled blue vesica/seed with an off-white core and a thin charcoal "crack" splitting the top tip (opening/germination).
Why it fits: a life beginning and compounding; solid filled shape punches at 32px; the crack is a subtle meaningful-gap / opening cue.
Con: vesica/seed shapes can read as a leaf or an eye depending on viewer; the crack is easy to lose at very small sizes.

### ab-07-facet-gem.svg — Faceted mandala
Concept: a bold filled blue hexagon with charcoal internal facet lines (inner hex + spokes to points) and an off-white core — many domains cut into one solid whole.
Why it fits: mandala/faceted "many resolving into one" without a loading-spinner look; degrades gracefully to a solid blue hex + white dot at 32px.
Con: a plain hexagon can feel a touch corporate/generic; the facet detail (its distinctive part) only reads at larger sizes.

### ab-08-negative-tile.svg — Negative-space "me"
Concept: a bold blue rounded-square app-tile with a charcoal circular void carved at center (the self as negative space), a small off-white core in the void, and a charcoal bite out of the top edge (the gap).
Why it fits: the only rounded-square silhouette in the set — distinctly "app," yet the self is defined by what's carved away; the edge-bite keeps the incomplete-whole theme.
Con: the tile-with-hole can read as a generic aperture/button; the top-edge bite is subtle and may vanish at 32px.

### ab-09-open-triangle.svg — One unbroken line, open apex
Concept: a single bold blue stroke tracing a soft rounded triangle that stops just short of closing at the top apex, around an off-white core.
Why it fits: one continuous life made of a few parts (three sides = domains meeting), left deliberately open — meaningful gap as an un-closed one-stroke form. Bold enough for 32px.
Con: rounded-triangle-with-gap could read as a generic "play/pause"-adjacent glyph if the gap is misjudged; needs the core to anchor the "self" meaning.
