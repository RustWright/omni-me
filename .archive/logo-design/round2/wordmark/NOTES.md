## Path-drawn letterforms (per brief, ≥2)
- wm-03 (om), wm-06 (m), wm-07 (o) are drawn as `<path>` letterforms.
- wm-02, wm-05, wm-08 are drawn from primitives (circle/rect), not `<text>`.
- Text-based: wm-01, wm-04.

## Pairing suggestion
Use wm-01 (or wm-04) as the horizontal/stacked wordmark, paired with wm-02, wm-05, or wm-08 as the square app icon.
