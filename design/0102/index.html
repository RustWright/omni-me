  • Updated for rulings 93 and 111–115. The day runs from your first activity plus the morning routine to the bedtime target less the evening routine (114). All-day events are one label line (111). What each one costs
  • A is the smallest and is exactly what research §4.4 proposed. The week and the goals, which the weekly and monthly rituals produce, are only reachable by asking in chat.
  • B shows each timescale in full, but it is three screens to design and keep honest, and a tab you have to remember. A 7-day grid does not fit 390 px, so the week becomes a list anyway.
  • C keeps today on top and squeezes the rest: the part of today already done, then the remaining days, then the month's goals. One screen and no navigation, but today loses its morning half to a summary line.
  • My recommendation is C. It beat A because the weekly and monthly rituals produce something you should be able to glance at without asking. It beat B because on a phone, scrolling down costs less than switching tabs, and the week fits as rows.
